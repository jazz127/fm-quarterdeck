Call out shadowed copies, modified clones, incompatible or unverified compatibility, and unknown integrity. Never call installed wrappers or native binaries for version discovery. Present it as review-only; never execute it or auto-install. Unknown/changed legacy artifacts require their original ownership/migration procedure; the command does not bypass refusal guards.
