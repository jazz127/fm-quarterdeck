# Governance: source preview

The repository owner is the initial maintainer and release decision owner for Quarterdeck. They approve changes, public releases, security responses and contributor policy. The owner may designate maintainers explicitly; no wider maintainer team, upstream sponsorship, review service or release schedule is promised. Public contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) after the designated public repository completes [maintainer setup](docs/OPEN-SOURCE-CONTRIBUTIONS.md). CI and a current maintainer review are required for integration; passing checks does not promise a merge. Conduct expectations and enforcement are in [CODE_OF_CONDUCT](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Quarterdeck is available under the [MIT License](LICENSE), including private, commercial and modified use with its required notices. The source-preview contribution policy does not restrict those license permissions. Contributors retain rights to their respective work; maintenance authority is not a claim to own upstream or third-party work.

This structure is informed by Firstmate's public posture, adapted for Quarterdeck rather than importing Firstmate's project-specific claims or workflows. See [third-party attribution](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md), [security](SECURITY.md) and [contributions](CONTRIBUTING.md).

A public source release is not approval to publish a private-data service. Release decisions must retain the reviewed source/export and explicit runtime-home boundaries in [public-source gates](docs/PUBLIC-SOURCE.md). Publication, hosting and changes to real operator data remain separate decisions.
